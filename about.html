A little about me

Code color + curiosity

I’m Kay S. H, a software developer who likes turning real-world problems into useful, maintainable software without losing sight of the people who have to use it.

How I Work

Practical systems
Thoughtful interfaces

My work spans production web applications, internal business tools, databases, APIs, authentication, third-party integrations, and polished user interfaces.

I like understanding the problem before reaching for a solution: how the data moves, what the workflow actually needs to support, where something can fail, and what will make the software easier to maintain later.

My design background still shapes the way I build. I care about clear hierarchy, responsive behavior, accessibility, and interfaces that make complicated systems feel straightforward rather than overwhelming.

What I Build

Software with a purpose

Recent work includes a production business website and staff CRM, collaborative TypeScript applications, searchable community tools, and open-source themes distributed through an established software ecosystem.

I’m especially interested in software that connects technical systems with real operational needs—tools that organize information, automate workflows, improve communication, or make a complicated process easier to use.
