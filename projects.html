Build a calm, trustworthy digital experience for a Florida technology-support business serving seniors and their families. The public site needed to explain services clearly, reduce jargon, and make requesting help feel straightforward.

A multi-page public website and staff-only CRM with customer and lead records, booking workflows, payment support, audit history, email tooling, and calendar integration.

Tools + Systems
